## Solar-house background

Use case: ads-marketing. Asset type: background artwork for a functional mobile solar-maintenance app, landscape 1536x1024. Recreate the background mood of the supplied SolarServe mobile UI designs: lush tropical Kerala garden, coconut palms, attractive modern two-storey cream house on the RIGHT half with a sloping dark roof densely covered with blue solar panels, warm golden morning sunlight at top right, richly saturated azure sky above. LEFT half mostly clean blue sky fading to dark teal vegetation, ample negative space for white UI headings. Photorealistic premium architectural photography, low three-quarter view, vivid yet credible sunlight, blue glass panels. House takes lower right two thirds with top half blue sky. No people, no vehicles, no text, no logos, no phone, no app interface, no borders. This is decorative artwork only, not a real worker/property record.
